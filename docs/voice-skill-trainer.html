Next: run it in Claude

Your work here is done. The rest happens in your own AI assistant.

  • Copy the prompt above.
  • Open Claude, a new chat, a Project, or the desktop app. It works just as well in ChatGPT, Copilot, or Gemini.
  • Paste the prompt and send. The assistant will analyze your samples and write your voice skill.
  • Save what it gives you. If you use Claude, save it as SKILL.md and you can upload it straight back as a Skill, or drop it into a Project. In other assistants, keep it as a text file and paste it in whenever you want that voice.

Then the payoff, try it: "Using my voice skill, write a short email declining a meeting." If it sounds like you, it works.

Runs entirely in your browser. Your writing never leaves this page until you paste the prompt into your AI assistant.
